Today

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

Today is a personal daily planner for tasks, habits, water intake, mood, focus sessions and saved links. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

The short version

  • Your data is stored on your device.
  • There is no Today account and no server operated by the developer.
  • There are no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • The optional AI assistant and optional cloud sync only send data to services you configure and control, using your credentials.
  • The developer does not receive your data.

Data stored on your device

Today keeps the following in a local database on your device:

  • Your display name, if you choose to enter one
  • Tasks, notes and due dates
  • Habits and habit check-ins
  • Water intake entries and goals
  • Mood entries (score, tags, notes)
  • Focus session history
  • Saved links, their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and your notes
  • Assistant chat history
  • App settings and reminder schedules

This data stays on your device unless you explicitly use one of the optional features below.

Optional features that send data off your device

AI assistant (opt-in, your own API key)

If you add your own API key for OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter or another compatible endpoint, the app sends your request directly from your device to that provider. A request can include:

  • Your message or command, and up to 8 recent chat messages
  • A small daily summary: water total, habit and task counts, mood score, focus totals, number of saved items, category names, and up to 8 recent saved-item titles
  • If you use voice input: a short audio recording, sent only to providers that support transcription (OpenAI and Groq)

Your API key is stored in the device's secure storage. The developer does not receive your prompts, recordings or summaries. The provider you choose handles that data under its own privacy policy.

Spoken replies use your device's text-to-speech engine. On some devices that engine may be provided by the platform, and the platform's policies apply. AI responses are generated by third-party models and may be inaccurate.

Cloud sync (opt-in, your own endpoint)

If you sign in with Apple or Google and configure your own sync endpoint, the app sends your synced records to that endpoint over HTTPS. Synced records include tasks, habits and check-ins, water entries, mood entries, focus sessions, saved links, categories and chat history. Your sign-in session is stored in secure storage. You operate and control that endpoint; the developer has no access to it.

Link previews

When you save a link, Today fetches its title, description and thumbnail from the website or its oEmbed provider. The URL you save is sent to that provider.

Opening links

Opening a saved link hands it to your device's browser or the app that handles that link.

Permissions

  • Microphone — only while you record a voice command or voice search.
  • Notifications — for the reminders and focus-session alerts you schedule. These notifications are local to your device; Today does not use push notifications.
  • Vibration — for haptic feedback.

Sharing and analytics

Today has no advertising, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no crash-reporting SDK. The developer does not sell or share your data, and does not operate any server that receives it.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings → Erase all data removes your local database contents.
  • Credentials: remove your API key in Assistant settings and sign out of cloud sync to delete the stored key and session token.
  • Everything: uninstalling Today removes all local app data, including secure storage.
  • Synced copies: data sent to your own sync endpoint or to an AI provider is deleted through those services, under their policies. The developer cannot access or delete it for you.

Children

Today is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect data from children.

Health information

Water, mood, habit and focus entries are self-tracking tools. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Changes to this policy
