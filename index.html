Todayly

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Todayly is a personal daily planner for tasks, habits, water intake, mood, focus sessions and saved links. This policy explains what data the app handles and where it goes.

The short version

  • Your data is stored on your device by default.
  • Cloud sync and the larger smart-assistant allowance are part of the optional Todayly Pro subscription ($4.99/month after a 7-day free trial). If you sign in with Google or create an email and password account, and turn it on, a copy of your synced records is stored in the app's managed backend.
  • There are no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • The optional AI assistant sends data only to the provider you configure, using your own API key.
  • The developer does not sell your data.

Data stored on your device

Todayly keeps the following in a local database on your device:

  • Your display name, email address and profile photo, if you choose to sign in with Google or create an account
  • Tasks, notes and due dates
  • Habits and habit check-ins
  • Water intake entries and goals
  • Mood entries (score, tags, notes)
  • Focus session history
  • Saved links, their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and your notes
  • Assistant chat history
  • App settings and reminder schedules

This data stays on your device unless you explicitly use one of the optional features below.

Optional features that send data off your device

AI assistant (opt-in, your own API key)

If you add your own API key for OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter or another compatible endpoint, the app sends your request directly from your device to that provider. A request can include:

  • Your message or command, and up to 8 recent chat messages
  • A small daily summary: water total, habit and task counts, mood score, focus totals, number of saved items, category names, and up to 8 recent saved-item titles
  • If you use voice input: a short audio recording, sent only to providers that support transcription (OpenAI and Groq)

Your API key is stored in the device's secure storage. The developer does not receive your prompts, recordings or summaries. The provider you choose handles that data under its own privacy policy.

Spoken replies use your device's text-to-speech engine. On some devices that engine may be provided by the platform, and the platform's policies apply. AI responses are generated by third-party models and may be inaccurate.

Cloud sync (opt-in, managed backend)

If you sign in and turn on cloud sync, Todayly stores a copy of your synced records in a managed backend hosted with Supabase so your devices can stay in sync. You can continue with Google, or create an email and password account. Synced records include tasks, habits and check-ins, water entries, mood entries, focus sessions, saved links, categories and assistant chat history. Google sign-in provides your email address, name and profile photo; an email account provides your email address. The app uses these to identify your account and show your profile. Passwords for email accounts are stored hashed by Supabase and are never readable by the developer. Data is sent over HTTPS.

The developer operates this backend and can access the stored records to provide and support cloud sync. The developer does not sell this data. You can turn cloud sync off at any time in Profile; your local data stays on your device.

Todayly Pro cost

Todayly Pro is an optional subscription costing $4.99 per month after a 7-day free trial, billed through Google Play. It includes cloud sync and the larger smart-assistant allowance. Google processes the payment; the app receives subscription status and expiry, not your payment card details. You can manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play.

Link previews

When you save a link, Todayly fetches its title, description and thumbnail from the website or its oEmbed provider. The URL you save is sent to that provider.

Opening links

Opening a saved link hands it to your device's browser or the app that handles that link.

Permissions

  • Microphone — only while you record a voice command or voice search.
  • Notifications — for the reminders and focus-session alerts you schedule. These notifications are local to your device; Todayly does not use push notifications.
  • Vibration — for haptic feedback.

Sharing and analytics

Todayly has no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The developer does not sell your data. Data is shared only with the services needed for the optional features you use: Supabase (cloud hosting), Google (sign-in), Google Play (Todayly Pro payments), and the AI or transcription provider used for smart answers.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Profile → Erase all data removes your local database contents.
  • Cloud sync / Todayly Pro: Profile → Todayly Pro → Delete account deletes your account and every synced copy. You can also turn cloud sync off to stop syncing. If you cannot open the app, email developer.saleheen@gmail.com from the account's email address and we delete the account and synced rows within 30 days.
  • Credentials: remove your API key in Assistant settings and sign out of cloud sync to delete the stored key and session token.
  • Everything: uninstalling Todayly removes all local app data, including secure storage.
  • AI provider data: data sent to an AI provider is deleted through that provider, under its policies.

Children

Todayly is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect data from children.

Health information

Water, mood, habit and focus entries are self-tracking tools. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
