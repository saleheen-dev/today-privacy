1. The app

Todayly is a personal daily planner for tasks, habits, water intake, mood, focus sessions and saved links. The app works locally on your device. An account is only required for optional Todayly Pro (cloud sync and the smart assistant).

Todayly does not provide medical advice. Water, mood and habit entries are for self-tracking only.
